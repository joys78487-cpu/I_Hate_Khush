I need you to read this the way it was written: slowly, and without preparing a response.

I cared about you. That part was real. I invested time, trust, and emotion into this connection because I believed there was something worth protecting. I didn't walk into this looking for pain. I walked into it hoping for understanding.

When things went wrong, I tried to be patient. I tried to listen. I tried to meet you where you were even when I felt unseen. But caring for someone doesn't mean I have to keep absorbing hurt until there's nothing left of me.

I was genuinely hurt, not performatively, not for attention, but in the quiet way hurt lives when you stop talking about it and start carrying it alone. That weight became too heavy. I'm tired of holding it while acting like I'm fine.

I don't want revenge. I don't want to humiliate you or turn this into something cruel. I don't want another confrontation where we talk in circles and end up in the same place. I have no interest in punishing you. I only want what I need: distance.

Distance so I can breathe. Distance so I can stop bracing for the next message, the next explanation, the next moment where I have to defend my feelings. Distance so I can heal without wondering if opening my door means opening old wounds.

I'm not asking you to agree with everything I've written. I'm asking you to respect that this is where I am, firmly, finally, and that my decision isn't a debate.

I hope you eventually understand why I had to walk away.

Take care of yourself. Just do it far away from me.
